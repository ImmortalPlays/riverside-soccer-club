About the club

More than a game — a Riverside tradition

Riverside Soccer Club is a volunteer-run community club focused on fun, skill-building, and belonging. We welcome every kid, from first-time players to future stars.

🌟

Everyone plays

Equal playing time and a positive, pressure-free environment for ages 5–14.

🎓

Real coaching

Licensed volunteer coaches who teach the fundamentals and good sportsmanship.

🤝

Community first

Affordable fees, financial aid, and a club run by parents, for families.
